Roadworks

We build roads that move communities, support trade, and stand up to daily use. Our roadworks service includes repair, rehabilitation, surface preparation, drainage support, and durable finishing.

Why road building matters

Roads connect people to work, school, healthcare, markets, and essential services. Strong roads reduce delays, lower transport costs, and increase safety for every road user.

Our approach

  • Proper site preparation and grading.
  • Drainage planning to prevent water damage.
  • Reliable compaction and material control.
  • Durable finishing for long-term performance.

Why clients choose GEITECH

We understand that roads are not only transport links but also civic infrastructure. Our team builds them to be safe, practical, and dependable from the first layer to the final finish.
